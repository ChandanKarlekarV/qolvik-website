Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Qolvik ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information this website collects, why, and the choices you have. It covers this website only — client products we build (such as a school's ERP) have their own agreements and policies.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address, the services you're interested in and your message.
  • Email: anything you choose to send when you write to us directly.
  • Anonymous analytics: page views, referring site, country and device type, collected without cookies and without identifying you.

We do not use advertising cookies, we do not sell your data, and we do not knowingly collect information from children through this website.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss a possible project.
  • To understand which pages are useful so we can improve the website.
  • To protect the website against spam and abuse.

Who processes it

We use trusted service providers to run this website: our website host (which also receives contact-form submissions), an email provider, privacy-friendly analytics, and Google Fonts (which receives your IP address when fonts load). They process data only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

Enquiries are kept for up to 24 months so we can follow up, then deleted unless we start working together. Anonymous analytics are kept in aggregate.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or withdraw your consent at any time. Email us at and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

This website is served only over HTTPS with modern security headers. Access to enquiries is limited to our team.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
