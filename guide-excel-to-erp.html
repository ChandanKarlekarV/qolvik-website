Moving from Excel to a school ERP in two weeks

A day-by-day plan for switching your school from spreadsheets to an ERP without disrupting classes — what to collect, clean, set up and test.

Switching from spreadsheets feels like a big project, but most of the work is getting your data ready. With one person in charge, two weeks is realistic for most schools.

Before you start: collect these files

  • Student list with class, section, admission number and date of birth
  • Parent names and mobile numbers
  • Fee structure, concessions and pending dues per student
  • Staff list, subjects and the current timetable
  • Last year's report card (to copy the format)

Name one person — usually the office administrator — as the owner of the switch.

Days 1–3: clean and import

  • One row per student, with no merged cells.
  • The same class names everywhere ("8-A", not "8A" in one sheet and "VIII A" in another).
  • Ten-digit mobile numbers, with no spaces or missing digits.
  • Remove duplicates and students who have left.

Days 4–7: set it up your way

Enter fee heads, instalments and concessions, your report-card format, and who can see what. Check every fee total against your spreadsheet.

Days 8–10: train in short sessions

Office staff first (admissions, fees, receipts), then teachers (attendance and marks). Thirty-minute sessions with a one-page guide work better than one long workshop.

Days 11–14: run both side by side

For a few days, keep the spreadsheet and the ERP running together and compare the numbers. When they match, invite parents to the app and switch the spreadsheet to read-only.

Tip: pick your moment

The start of a term is the easiest time to switch. Avoid exam and results weeks.
