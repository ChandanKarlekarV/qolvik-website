Growth through colleges

Partner institution programme

Colleges and trusts with strong networks bring the next customers. Give them a better price and a transparent referral reward.

What a partner gets

  • Partner price on every package, locked for 2 years
  • 5% referral credit on the first-year value of every institution they refer that signs, applied against their own AMC or subscription
  • Bundle discount: 10% off a website when ordered with an ERP
  • Founding partners (first 3 institutions): an extra 6 months of support, in return for a case study, testimonial and reference calls

Rules that keep it clean

  • Rewards go to the institution, on an invoice or as credit, never as cash to an individual. This matters for government and aided colleges.
  • The Partner price is the lowest negotiated deal. The Floor is only for reduced scope, not favours.
  • A referral counts once the new institution pays its first milestone.
  • Put the programme in writing in the partner's contract.
Contracts

Payment terms and bargaining rules

Payment milestones

  • Websites and design: 50% advance, 50% before go-live
  • MVPs and apps: 40% advance, 30% at the beta demo, 30% before go-live
  • ERP: 30% advance, 30% at the module demo, 30% at go-live, 10% after 30 stable days
  • Source code and IP transfer to the client on final payment
  • Quotes are valid for 30 days. Scope is a written feature list; anything else is a change request.
  • Two revision rounds per design stage

GST: add 18% once Qolvik is GST-registered. Registration is required above ₹20 lakh yearly turnover for services. GST is paid to the government, so it is never profit. Confirm with a CA.

How to bargain without losing money

  1. Open with the List price.
  2. Aim to close at Standard.
  3. Give a discount only in exchange for something: full payment up front (−5%), a case study, a referral, or a longer timeline.
  4. Below the Floor, cut scope instead: fewer pages, fewer modules, or move features to phase 2.
  5. Never include unlimited changes or unlimited support.
